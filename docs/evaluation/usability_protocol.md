# Protocol: criterion 10 (usability)

**Framework:** PACMAD (Harrison, Flood & Duce, 2013) attributes measured: effectiveness (task
completion), efficiency (time on task), errors, learnability (unaided first use) and cognitive load
(SUS or the MARS subscales). This answers the M1 feedback asking for a named usability model.

**Participants:** 5. Check with the course lecturer whether peer testing needs AUTEC ethics approval.
Do not use participants' own medicines. Give each participant a prepared kit of product boxes.

**Tasks** (unaided, think-aloud, timed):

1. Add the three products in the kit by scanning their labels.
2. One label produces an ambiguous match. Choose the correct ingredient.
3. Check for interactions and say what the most serious one is.
4. Explain in your own words what the app is telling you to do. Check that the participant
   recognises the result as information, not a diagnosis (R8).

**Instruments:** SUS (10 items) or the MARS app-quality subscales (Stoyanov et al., 2015), plus
observer notes on errors and hesitations.

**Pass (M1):** all 5 participants complete the tasks unaided, and all 5 identify the output as informational.
